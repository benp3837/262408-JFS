  "text": "Reporting Officer Deputy H. Mullins Date of Incident October 14th Location Bramblewood Estates, Unit 7B At approximately 11:15 PM, this department received a noise complaint from a Mrs. Edna Kowalski regarding what she described as 'an ungodly amount of hissing' coming from the unit directly above hers. Upon arrival, Deputies Mullins and Ferreira found the front door of Unit 7B unlocked and partially open. Inside, the living room had been rearranged in a manner consistent with a large creature having moved through it at speed. The entertainment center was knocked over. A ceiling fan had been removed from the ceiling and placed on the couch, still running on the lowest setting.The tenant, one Gerald P. Hoffsteader, was located in the kitchen, seated on top of the refrigerator. Mr. Hoffsteader reported that he had returned home from his evening walk to find a vampire standing in his living room, helping itself to items from his refrigerator. He described the vampire as approximately six feet tall, wearing what he called 'an extremely dated waistcoat,' and carrying a reusable grocery bag. Mr. Hoffsteader noted that the vampire was not aggressive but was, in his words, 'extremely judgmental' about the contents of his refrigerator, specifically the brand of oat milk he purchases.Deputies attempted to locate the vampire but found only a trail of refrigerator magnets leading to an open window on the north side of the unit. The grocery bag was recovered and found to contain one block of medium cheddar, a sleeve of crackers, and a small jar of fig jam. No blood was consumed at the scene. Mr. Hoffsteader was unharmed but described himself as 'deeply rattled and also a little insulted.' No charges were filed. The refrigerator magnets were returned to Mr. Hoffsteader upon request. The ceiling fan was reattached by building maintenance the following morning. This department recommends that Mr. Hoffsteader invest in a deadbolt and possibly reconsider his oat milk brand, though the latter is outside our jurisdiction. Follow-up notes: A similar incident was reported at a nearby grocery store two days later, where a figure matching Mr. Hoffsteader's description was observed in the cheese aisle before vanishing when a store employee approached. The reusable bag was not recovered.",
