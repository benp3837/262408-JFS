  "text": "INCIDENT REPORT - CASE FILE 0117 Reporting Officer: Officer D. Yamamoto Date of Incident: November 2nd Location: Pinebrook Public Library, Main Branch At 2:30 PM, library staff contacted this department after a ghost was observed in the periodicals section on the second floor. The ghost, described by multiple witnesses as translucent, approximately middle-aged in appearance, and wearing business casual attire, had been present since the library opened at 9:00 AM and had so far not caused any disturbance. Staff called only after the ghost began sighing audibly, which was disrupting patrons attempting to read. Upon arrival, Officer Yamamoto made contact with the ghost, who identified itself as the spirit of one Leonard Fitch, former assistant branch manager, deceased 2009. Mr. Fitch stated that he had returned because he was, in his words, not at peace with how the Dewey Decimal reorganization was handled in 2021. He had specific grievances about the relocation of the 600s section, which he felt had been placed in a high-traffic area not suited to technical reference materials. Officer Yamamoto consulted with current branch manager Patricia Osei, who agreed to review the 600s placement in the upcoming facilities meeting. Upon being informed of this, Mr. Fitch expressed satisfaction, thanked Officer Yamamoto, apologized for the sighing, and dissipated at approximately 3:15 PM. A faint smell of copy toner was noted at the site of dissipation and cleared within the hour. No property damage occurred. Two patrons requested refunds on their late fees as emotional distress compensation, which was denied. Ms. Osei noted for the record that the 600s section placement is a matter that has honestly been bothering her too. This case is considered resolved. Officer Yamamoto has been commended for her calm handling of the situation. Mr. Fitch has not returned as of the date of this report, though a reference book on municipal zoning was found in the wrong section the following morning. This may or may not be related.",
