  "text": "INCIDENT REPORT - CASE FILE 0089 Reporting Officer: Sergeant T. Braddock Date of Incident: October 31st Location: Pinecrest Elementary School, Gymnasium This report documents the events of the annual Pinecrest Elementary Halloween carnival, which was disrupted at approximately 7:40 PM by the presence of what witnesses have described as a genuine werewolf. The creature entered through the gymnasium's emergency exit, which had been propped open by a cooler of juice boxes. It is unclear how the cooler got there. The werewolf, estimated at eight feet tall in its current form, moved through the carnival in a manner that initially caused no alarm, as many attendees assumed it was a costume. This assumption held for approximately four minutes, during which the creature consumed eleven hot dogs from the concession stand, accepted a participation ribbon from the costume contest judges who placed it third behind a child dressed as a pirate and a child dressed as a pirate's parrot, and attempted to use the photo booth, which it was too large to enter. Panic began when the creature sneezed, which knocked over the beanbag toss and startled a therapy dog named Biscuit, who began barking. The werewolf, apparently embarrassed, attempted to back out of the gymnasium and became briefly stuck in the doorframe. Several parents documented this on their phones. The creature freed itself and departed into the adjacent soccer field at approximately 7:52 PM. No injuries were reported among attendees. One folding table was destroyed. The photo booth was undamaged. The participation ribbon was not recovered and is believed to still be in the creature's possession. Principal Nguyen has asked that this report reflect that the carnival was otherwise a success and that the hot dog situation has been addressed with the vendor. The therapy dog Biscuit has been cleared for future events following a brief recovery period. This department has no further leads on the creature's identity or current whereabouts. A size 22 partial paw print was recovered from the soccer field and is being held as evidence.",
